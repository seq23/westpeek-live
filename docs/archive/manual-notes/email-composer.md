# Writing to a group — the email composer

**What changed:** you can email a group. Before this you could email seven named individuals, one
address at a time, from seven boxes on an event's Communications page. There was no way to reach
all your attendees, your VIPs, your speakers, your sponsors, your crew or your client.

**Where it is:** the Email tab → **Write to a group**, or go straight to `/app/email/compose`.
The links next to your speaker list, sponsor list and attendee roster open the same screen with the
event and the group already filled in.

---

## The four fields

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Event** | A picker, not a place. Arriving from a roster link fills it in. Leave it on *No event* to write to one person across events — that is allowed on purpose, not by accident. |
| **To** | A group, not an address: all registered attendees · VIPs · speakers · sponsors · crew · the client · one person. |
| **Message** | One of the seven templates you already send, or write your own subject and body. |
| **Send** | One press. The line above the button names the group and the number. |

Press **Work out who that is** and the screen tells you how many people that is before anything is
sent — and **Show me exactly who** lists every name and address. Use it. A wrong audience cannot be
taken back once it has gone.

## Where each group actually comes from

- **Attendees** — everyone with an active registration for that event.
- **VIPs** — current VIP grants. Rotating the VIP code drops everyone admitted under the old one,
  and they drop out of this group too.
- **Speakers, sponsors, the client** — the people who came in through their role code and told us
  who they are.
- **Crew** — the contractors **booked** or **paid** on that event. A shortlisted name is somebody you
  have not hired, so they are not on a crew call.

**New:** speakers, sponsors, VIPs and clients are now asked for an email address when they first
enter through their role code, the same way they are asked for their company. Before this they had
no address anywhere in the system, which is why "Email speakers" could not exist. **Anyone who
entered before today has no address on file** — the composer counts them and says so rather than
pretending the group is smaller than it is. They will be reachable once they next open their portal
and fill it in.

## What it refuses to do

- **An empty group.** It will not tell you it sent something to nobody.
- **A group that changed while you were writing.** If somebody registered or unsubscribed between the
  count you read and the button you pressed, nothing is sent and you re-read the new number.
- **More than today's allowance.** Roughly 100 messages a day and 3,000 a month on this plan. A send
  that would cross the line is refused whole — nothing is ever half-sent, because there would be no
  way afterwards to tell who got it.

## Unsubscribe

Every group message carries an unsubscribe link, and the headers that make Gmail and Apple Mail show
their own unsubscribe button. This is not optional: the moment you email a group it is bulk email and
your domain's reputation is on the line.

- **An unsubscribe is for the whole of West Peek**, not one event. Somebody who stops your summit
  announcements stops all of them.
- **They are dropped from every future group send**, and the count on the composer shows it:
  *"47 people · 2 unsubscribed"*.
- **Messages addressed to one person are never suppressed.** A speaker who unsubscribed from
  announcements still gets their own green room link, their tech-check nudge and their report. Those
  are not announcements and the unsubscribe list does not touch them.
- **Someone who did it by mistake** can put themselves back on from the same page, with no login. You
  can also do it for them from the **Unsubscribed** list under the composer.

## The volume figure

The number on the Email tab is counted from **this app's own send log**, and the panel says so.
Resend publishes no usage figure we can read, so the count is a floor: anything sent through Resend
from somewhere other than this app is not in it.

## What has not changed

**Nothing sends without you pressing a button.** There is no timer, no cron, no queue and no
automatic announcement anywhere in this product, and the composer did not introduce one.
