# Validator Risk Reclassification

Repo: `agency`

## Final classification

- HARD FAIL: 14
- STRONG WARNING: 30
- WARNING: 5
- INFO / NO VALIDATION: 5

## Policy

- HARD FAIL is reserved for real security, authorization, data, cleanup, build/deploy artifact, and critical-journey risks.
- Documentation and historical evidence never hard-fail by themselves.
- Live-only proof is a completion gate, not a local structural-failure claim.
- Duplicate aliases have no independent blocker authority.
